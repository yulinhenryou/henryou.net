Hello, brand-new me.

Henry Ou

2026 feels like a turning point—the most meaningful new beginning I’ve had in five years. I’m coming out of a difficult period with a clearer sense of direction and the energy to move forward.

From today, this website will be a place to get to know me: my background, what I care about, and the direction I’m taking. You can read about my interests and experiences, explore my projects, find my CV, or get in touch.

In Updates, I’ll share what I’m learning, ideas I’m working through, and moments from everyday life, in English or Chinese. Over time, I hope these pages give others a clearer sense of who I am, while keeping a record of what I’ve made, what I’ve learned, and how my thinking has changed.

There is a lot I want to understand, and a lot I want to create. I believe each year ahead will be better than the last. My part is to keep learning, keep building, and give that belief something to stand on.
